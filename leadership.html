My Leadership Experience

BU Board Games Club (10/2023 - 12/2025)

I served as the Secretary for Boston University's Board Games Club. My main role was to scout and book rooms for events, but I also helped in event planning (and acting!).

These are some of our main events:

Murder Mystery

Audience members enjoy dinner and a watch a murder mystery play. As the scenes unfold, they must gather clues from handmade puzzles to figure out who's the murderer!

Trivia Night

Teams of 3-10 answer live trivia questions, with topics ranging from "who's that silhouette?" to "guess the song from this guitar cover."

Duos Night

Annual Valentine's Day event where people form pairs and play 2-player board games. Bring a friend, or maybe find a special someone along the way.
